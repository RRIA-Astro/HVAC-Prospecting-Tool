HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.7
================================

Detector model: unchanged frozen v0.0.12
Primary thresholds: unchanged 0.07 / 0.35 / 0.45
Shifted-rescue Stage-1 floor: unchanged 0.015
Zoomed-perimeter Stage-1 floor: unchanged 0.008

1. Packaged-equipment precision
- Context-rejects a package hypothesis when both total probability is below 0.55 and best package
  probability is below 0.35. This removes weak generator/dumpster/vehicle promotions such as 1243
  Bayne and 189 Rosemont.
- Context-rejects package boxes from overview or parcel-tile views when they are more than 5 ft from
  a mapped building. A previously protected very-high-certainty exception remains for a box within
  15 ft with p>=0.95 and best class>=0.75. This removes the loading canopy at 2682 Dean and the
  semi-trailer beside Hobby Lobby without breaking the established 117.9 ft package control.
- A seam-cut package can rank only when the raw audit contains a non-seam observation of the same
  machine with p>=0.80 and best class>=0.65. If all ranked package representatives are seam-cut,
  they can create REVIEW but not STRONG. This surfaces the two real large units at 484 Viking as
  REVIEW instead of discarding both.
- One on-building, non-rescue package at least 25 ft with p>=0.65 and best class>=0.55 can now create
  REVIEW. This narrowly surfaces the 26 ft packaged AHU at 777 Seahawk.

2. Thermal-rescue precision
- On a property whose largest building is below 8,000 ft2, rescue-only thermal evidence more than
  45 ft from a mapped building is context-rejected. This removes the pallet/debris cluster at 2856
  Crusader.
- A compact review-only rescue box below 22 ft is rejected only when p<0.70 and best class<0.40.
  This removes the residential-style perimeter condenser at 2697 International while retaining the
  established 5580 Shell, 5925 Thurston, and 1444 Diamond Springs rescue controls.

3. Confidence-honest review routing
- A compact non-rescue rooftop cooling-tower label below 30 ft on a Commercial building of at least
  100,000 ft2 remains visible but cannot create STRONG by itself. Hobby Lobby therefore moves from
  STRONG to REVIEW; the current frozen classifier cannot safely distinguish that four-fan RTU from
  every legitimate compact rooftop heat-rejection unit.
- A military/federal property with a building of at least 30,000 ft2 and at least one unverified
  normal Stage-1 proposal can receive REVIEW after all verified equipment paths are empty. The row
  explicitly reports "Strategic-site unverified proposal" and does not claim a tower or chiller.
  This surfaces NAS Oceana - NEXCOM HQ while honestly preserving the model's fanless-equipment blind
  spot.

4. Diagnostic replay

- 2682 Dean, 2697 International, 2856 Crusader, 1243 Bayne, and 189 Rosemont move to QUIET.
- 777 Seahawk, NAS Oceana - NEXCOM HQ, and 484 Viking move to REVIEW.
- Hobby Lobby moves from STRONG to REVIEW.
