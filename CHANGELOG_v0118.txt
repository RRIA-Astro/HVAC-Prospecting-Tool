HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.8 — Norfolk Expansion
==================================================
Date: 2026-09-18

- Adds Norfolk/Virginia Beach city selection and per-property source routing.
- Norfolk: public city address points, parcel polygons, building polygons, and 2025 imagery.

- Protects city/discovery/scan state so source selection cannot change mid-request.
