HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.8 — Validation Notes
=================================================

The build's automated test command is:
  python -m unittest -v test_detection_logic.py test_territories.py test_release_contract.py

Result before packaging: 62 tests passed. Workflow YAML parsed successfully and release_identity.py
reported APP_VERSION=0.11.8, APP_TAG=v0118, APP_NAME=HVAC_Territory_Discovery_v0118.

Frozen detection checks
-----------------------
The full inherited v0.11.7 regression suite checks the known package/thermal controls, 5925 zoom
coverage, small-priority rescue, strategic near-miss REVIEW routing, storage morphology, and
cross-view/property de-duplication. Tests assert DETECTOR_BASELINE_VERSION instead of a stale
APP_VERSION literal. A separate contract checks all retained model assets and baseline inference,
ranking, prescreen/geometry helper code and constants against the v0.11.7 source.

Norfolk adapter checks
----------------------
Tests cover the separate city endpoints, 4326 geometry, strict radius, GPIN normalization,
batched/paginated assessment queries, multi-part parcels, residential exclusions, commercial-condo
ownership, self-storage, institutional/industrial/utility priorities, improvement-vs-land use,
ambiguous address rejection, no silent city fallback, missing-assessment auditing, demolished
footprint filtering, footprint fallback, and complete footprint-service failure.

Acquisition/output checks
-------------------------
City exports use identical projected frames and pixel counts. Every campus view routes by its
property's territory. Wrong-sized, completely blank, and ArcGIS error exports stop before inference.
Scan/source metadata, CSV fields, cap disclosure, and app-derived release names are tested.

Live source smoke tests
-----------------------
Official Norfolk service metadata and actual city queries were checked on 2026-09-18. The City Hall
address resolves to approximately -76.284637, 36.847094. A 0.5-mile discovery exercise found 318
GPIN-grouped parcels, zero unmatched FY27 assessment GPINs, and usable NORFOLK CITY building polygons.
The final adapter recheck returned 280 current building polygons, 250 joined footprints, 317 candidate
rows before the cap, and 76 prescreen-passing properties. All 76 passing candidates fit under the
250 displayed-row cap; only nonpassing rows were omitted. No assessment matches were missing and
the NORFOLK CITY source was used without fallback. Counts can change as city data is updated.

A real 2025 MapServer export at 1800 x 1800 pixels was downloaded and visually inspected. It is
usable aerial imagery, not a basemap/error page. No automatic switch to lower-quality/older imagery
is implemented. The final downloader also retrieved and validated a real 1800 x 1800 Norfolk export.
This is a source check, not an independent audit of native sensor resolution.

Limits
------
Offline regressions and source smoke checks are not a full Norfolk detector validation. The scratch
environment lacks torch/torchvision/ultralytics and a desktop display. Windows PyInstaller assembly
and full model inference are to be exercised through the GitHub-built EXE and Richard's blind scan.
Headless UI-state tests exercise initialization, city switching, control locks, completed/failed scan
output, and unlocking after failure; they do not substitute for a visual Windows GUI check.
Tall-building shadows/roof displacement, outdated/incomplete geometry, hidden/fanless equipment,
and classifier lookalikes remain limitations of the frozen baseline. No changes were made for the
3432 Virginia Beach construction outlier or other reviewed detector misses in this release.
