Live source smoke tests

GPIN-grouped parcels, zero unmatched FY27 assessment GPINs, and usable NORFOLK CITY building polygons.

A real 2025 MapServer export at 1800 x 1800 pixels was downloaded and visually inspected. It is
usable aerial imagery, not a basemap/error page. No automatic switch to lower-quality/older imagery
is implemented. The final downloader also retrieved and validated a real 1800 x 1800 Norfolk export.
This is a source check, not an independent audit of native sensor resolution.

Limits
------
Offline regressions and source smoke checks are not a full Norfolk detector validation. The scratch
environment lacks torch/torchvision/ultralytics and a desktop display. Windows PyInstaller assembly
and full model inference are to be exercised through the GitHub-built EXE and Richard's blind scan.
Headless UI-state tests exercise initialization, city switching, control locks, completed/failed scan
output, and unlocking after failure; they do not substitute for a visual Windows GUI check.
Tall-building shadows/roof displacement, outdated/incomplete geometry, hidden/fanless equipment,
and classifier lookalikes remain limitations of the frozen baseline. No changes were made for the
3432 Virginia Beach construction outlier or other reviewed detector misses in this release.
