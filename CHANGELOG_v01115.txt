HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.15 — Portsmouth Expansion
=========================================================

Added
-----
* Portsmouth is available in the city selector.
* Official Portsmouth address, assessor-enriched parcel, and building-footprint routes.
* Exact Portsmouth address matching with duplicate and suite-record preference controls.
* Portsmouth parcel use, building type, owner, zoning, neighborhood, assessed area, and raw audit fields.
* High-resolution Portsmouth 2022 municipal aerial tile mosaics with bounded concurrency and local caching.
* Portsmouth regression coverage for routing, joins, imagery mosaics, fail-closed footprints, selector state,
  source metadata, and the existing large-medical-campus manual-review safety.

Changed
-------
* Suffolk is hidden from the deployment selector after its blind scan showed unacceptable false negatives.
  Its adapter and tests remain available for diagnostics and a future imagery replacement.

Safeguards
----------
* No model asset, primary confidence threshold, inference pass, or existing-city ranking rule changed.
* Tile frames stop before inference if any tile is missing/unreadable, if a tile is not 256 x 256, if the
  request exceeds 100 tiles, or if the completed frame is blank.
* Missing city and fallback footprints stop discovery before prescreening.
* Chesapeake and Newport News remain hidden pending better imagery.
