HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.15 — Validation Notes
=====================================================

Release scope
-------------
Portsmouth acquisition, tiled imagery, and territory routing only. The v0.11.7 detector baseline and
v0.0.12 model assets are unchanged. The only protected ranking extension adds Portsmouth to the existing
context-only manual-review safety for medical campuses with a building at least 100,000 ft2.

Offline coverage
----------------
The release suite verifies:
* exact Portsmouth address parsing and official address-layer routing;
* duplicate and suite address preference on a shared parcel;
* self-contained parcel assessment context and FID-based pagination order;
* auditable building type, property type, owner, classification, zoning, and assessed area;
* official Portsmouth building footprints and fail-closed fallback behavior;
* bounded tile-mosaic routing, output dimensions, and the absence of a nonexistent export dependency;
* selector isolation, scan metadata, and source audit fields;
* Portsmouth coverage for the existing context-only large-medical-campus manual-review safety;
* all prior frozen detector and city regression controls.

Live service validation — 2026-09-21
------------------------------------
Exact address search succeeded for 3636 High St. A 0.5-mile discovery returned 1,208 parcels and
1,881 official Portsmouth building footprints, joined 1,764 footprints, and found 33 prescreen-passing
properties. The general candidate inventory exceeded 250 rows, but prescreen-passing properties sort
first and all 33 were displayed.

The Portsmouth municipal Aerials_2022 service advertises cached detail through 0.0746 projected meters
per pixel. The app intentionally selects a bounded level that produces about 1,200 native pixels per
frame, then resizes the exact crop to the detector's frozen 1,800-pixel contract. This avoids hundreds of
tile requests per view while preserving visibly useful equipment detail. Tiles are cached locally so
overlapping property views and later scans do not download them again.

Maryview Medical Center retained clear rooftop and mechanical-yard detail in the production frame.
Churchland and mixed urban samples had consistent resolution. Bright white roofs can still reduce local
contrast, so the first deployment test should remain blind and include QUIET spot checks.

Recommended field test
----------------------
Run a genuinely new Portsmouth area at 0.5 mile and 10,000 ft2. Review every STRONG and REVIEW result,
spot-check QUIET sites, and return prospecting_results.csv plus individual folders for definite misses or
confusing false positives. Initial imagery acquisition may be slower while the tile cache is populated.
