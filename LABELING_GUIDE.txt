HVAC AERIAL LABELING GUIDE

Goal: label equipment that would make a commercial HVAC salesperson stop and investigate.
Do not try to catalog every rooftop object.

COOLING TOWER
Box the tower/fluid cooler/evaporative heat-rejection unit itself. Single-cell and multi-cell units both count.

AIR-COOLED CHILLER
Box the complete chiller chassis. Favor genuinely large multi-fan/finned equipment over small condenser banks.

LARGE PACKAGED HVAC
Use only for genuinely large packaged RTU/AHU equipment that is interesting from a commercial service standpoint.
Do not use this label for small ordinary rooftop units.

PROCESS/HYDRONIC PIPING
Box a meaningful visible piping run/cluster where its routing/scale is useful evidence. It is okay for the box to
cover the visible connected run rather than tracing the pipe pixel by pixel.

MECHANICAL YARD / PROCESS
Box a coherent mechanical/process area when the overall arrangement is important even if individual machines are
hard to identify.

OTHER HIGH-VALUE MECHANICAL
Use sparingly for clearly valuable mechanical equipment that does not fit the other classes.

NEGATIVE IMAGE
Mark Negative when NONE of the target high-value classes is present. Ordinary small condensers, splits, small RTUs,
exhaust fans, vents and clean roofs are good negative examples. These negative images are important for preventing
the future detector from repeating the false positives seen during generic-model testing.

CONSISTENCY
- Box the full visible equipment item when practical.
- If equipment is partly occluded, box the visible footprint reasonably.
- Do not box the same object multiple times in one image.
- Multiple views of the same equipment are acceptable; the dataset should later be split by SITE, not by image,
  so nearly identical images do not leak between training and validation sets.
