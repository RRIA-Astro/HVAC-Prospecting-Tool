HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.13 — Hampton Expansion
=====================================================
Date: 2026-09-20

- Adds Hampton exact address lookup, official address/place-name enrichment, official parcel and
  Real Estate improvement joins, official building footprints/types, and source auditing.
- Uses Hampton's 2026 municipal aerial layer at the established projected frame, physical side
  length, and 1800-pixel detector input.
- Prefers the strongest active nonresidential improvement on multi-building Hampton campuses so a
  dormitory or residential record cannot erase hospital, college, laboratory, or commercial context.
- Excludes Hampton pools, tanks, towers, and sheds from building-envelope joins while retaining
  nonresidential, residential, trailer-home, and garage structures.
- Stops Hampton discovery before prescreening when required building footprints are unavailable,
  preventing a data-service outage from generating misleading QUIET results.
- Extends the existing context-only >=100,000-ft2 medical-campus manual-review route to Hampton.
- Hides Newport News and Chesapeake from the selectable deployment cities after field scans showed
  their available orthophotos were not reliable enough for blind prospecting. Their adapters remain
  in source for future recovery and regression.
- Preserves Norfolk and Virginia Beach detector behavior and all existing field controls.
- Retains the complete 22,410,981-byte ResNet18 checkpoint.

No model was retrained. Model hashes and primary thresholds remain unchanged.
