HVAC Local Detector v0.0.12 — Model Card
=========================================
Intended use: commercial-HVAC sales prospecting triage from high-resolution Virginia Beach aerial imagery.

not a bad imagery download. The release adds Norfolk-only building-footprint attribution capped at
REVIEW, distinct-building rescue coverage, and a bounded high-value public-site near-miss REVIEW.

v0.11.8 checkpoint repair: restores the complete original v0.11.7 FP16 embedder (22,410,981 bytes;
SHA256 7dbdd679df66598a8d9e63f983507eb7900af9553595a3bb250ee6e4795e6ffd). The first Norfolk package

External Round-4 blind validation: 40 fresh properties; 18 target-positive; 22 clean negative. Strict localized positive-property recall 88.9% (16/18); property surface recall 94.4% (17/18); negative-property FPR 22.7% (5/22). This is a prospecting filter, not an engineering survey or inventory. Cooling towers remain the weakest class. QUIET is not proof of absence.

Packaging note: v0.11.7 continues to store ResNet18 weights in FP16 and loads them into the normal FP32 network at runtime. Detector weights and frozen thresholds are unchanged from v0.11.1. v0.11.7 changes deterministic ranking and review routing rather than retraining the detector.


v0.11.5 field logic note:
The underlying v0.0.12 Stage-1 and verifier weights remain unchanged. Primary thresholds remain 0.07 / 0.35 / 0.45. The existing shifted rescue retains its 0.015 Stage-1 floor. v0.11.5 adds a final zoomed perimeter rescue only for eligible priority properties when normal scanning has no tower/chiller hypothesis and shifted rescue has no rankable thermal evidence. It enlarges a bounded 3 x 3 grid of 512-pixel crops to 1024 pixels and uses a Stage-1 floor of 0.008. Retained zoomed evidence requires total thermal probability >=0.55 and best thermal-class probability >=0.50.

All low-Stage-1 rescue detections are REVIEW-only, even when the Stage-2 verifier likes them. The Stage-2 numeric candidate-confidence feature is floored at 0.07 only for rescue scoring. Normal detections are unchanged.

Thermal STRONG triage remains confidence-aware. v0.11.5 additionally context-rejects weak overview seams, distant rescue evidence, broad low-confidence overview boxes, and storage-like morphology while preserving raw detections for audit. This is ranking logic only; it does not alter model weights or normal detector thresholds.

v0.11.6 field logic note:
Normal and rescue inference are byte-for-byte unchanged from v0.11.5. The prescreen now excludes
small ambiguous Public/Semi Public parcels unless they have explicit institutional/utility identity.
Ranking rejects broad near-square normal thermal boxes when p<0.55 and best class<0.30. Batch scans
also reconcile near-identical ordinary-parcel thermal detections across properties and retain the
losing detection in the JSON audit. Campus-adjacent evidence is not cross-property reconciled.

v0.11.7 field logic note:
Normal and rescue inference remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Ranking now rejects weak singleton
package hypotheses and off-building package boxes from wide views, with a narrow protected
high-certainty exception. Seam-cut package evidence requires a strong non-seam same-machine
observation and is REVIEW-only when it is the only package path. Small-site distant rescue clutter
and weak compact rescue boxes are rejected. Compact rooftop tower labels on very large Commercial
buildings are REVIEW-capped. A bounded military/federal near-miss route can surface an unverified
Stage-1 proposal without asserting an HVAC class. General fanless heat-rejection recognition remains
outside the frozen model's demonstrated capabilities.
