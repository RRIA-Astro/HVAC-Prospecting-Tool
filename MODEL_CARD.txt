HVAC Local Detector v0.0.12 — Model Card
=========================================
Intended use: commercial-HVAC sales prospecting triage from high-resolution Virginia Beach aerial imagery.

Frozen thresholds: candidate 0.07; tower/chiller 0.35; large packaged 0.45.

External Round-4 blind validation: 40 fresh properties; 18 target-positive; 22 clean negative. Strict localized positive-property recall 88.9% (16/18); property surface recall 94.4% (17/18); negative-property FPR 22.7% (5/22). Class localized recall: tower 66.7% (6/9), chiller 100% (6/6), large packaged 91.7% (11/12).

This is a prospecting filter, not an engineering survey or inventory. Cooling towers remain the weakest class. QUIET is not proof of absence.

Packaging note: v0.11.4 continues to store ResNet18 weights in FP16 and loads them into the normal FP32 network at runtime. Detector weights and frozen thresholds are unchanged from v0.11.1. v0.11.4 keeps the detector weights frozen and adds a recall-only deep-rescue proposal path plus thermal triage refinement rather than retraining the detector.


v0.11.4 field logic note:
The underlying v0.0.12 Stage-1 and verifier weights remain unchanged. Primary thresholds remain 0.07 / 0.35 / 0.45. v0.11.4 adds a separate deep thermal rescue only when a high-value property has no retained tower/chiller evidence. Rescue uses shifted overlap tiles and a Stage-1 proposal floor of 0.015. Proposals below the frozen 0.07 Stage-1 operating point are always REVIEW-only, even if the Stage-2 visual verifier likes them. The Stage-2 numeric candidate-confidence feature is floored at 0.07 only for this REVIEW rescue scoring. Normal detections are unchanged.

Thermal STRONG triage is also confidence-aware: compact high-confidence chillers can be STRONG, while physically large but low-confidence tower/chiller lookalikes remain REVIEW. This is ranking logic only; it does not alter model weights or normal detector thresholds.
