HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.4
================================

Detector model: unchanged frozen v0.0.12
Primary thresholds: unchanged 0.07 / 0.35 / 0.45
Deep-rescue Stage-1 proposal floor: 0.015 (new; rescue only)
Thermal REVIEW verifier floor: 0.22 (rescue only)

Field-driven changes after the v0.11.3 Virginia Beach rerun:

1. Deep tower/chiller rescue for Stage-1 misses
- 5925 Thurston remained QUIET in v0.11.3 even though a tower/fluid-cooler-like unit was clearly visible in a MECH FOCUS image.
- Diagnosis: normal 1024-pixel corner tiles placed the side-yard object near inference-tile edges, while the single centered rescue tile did not cover it. Stage 1 therefore never produced a usable proposal on the object.
- v0.11.4 adds five shifted overlap tiles on each rescue view: top-middle, bottom-middle, left-middle, right-middle, and center.
- These tiles move perimeter equipment away from inference seams and change local context without changing normal scanning.
- Rescue Stage 1 runs at confidence 0.015 with max 32 proposals per tile. Only physically plausible 7-130 ft proposals are sent to Stage 2, capped at 14 per tile.
- A rescue proposal below the frozen 0.07 Stage-1 operating point is always REVIEW-only. It can never create STRONG evidence.
- For REVIEW scoring only, the Stage-2 numeric Stage-1-confidence feature is floored at 0.07 so the visual embedding can judge weak proposals rather than automatically rejecting them because of the low proposal score.

2. Thermal STRONG triage refinement
- v0.11.3 used a simple >=22 ft physical-size rule for direct tower/chiller STRONG evidence.
- That kept real compact chillers such as 1245 Miller Store at REVIEW and allowed large low-confidence lookalikes such as 1400 Air Rail landscaping and 1609 Diamond Springs clutter to appear STRONG.
- v0.11.4 makes STRONG thermal evidence confidence-aware. A direct non-rescue detection is STRONG when either:
  * total thermal probability >=0.78 and best thermal class probability >=0.40, or
  * long dimension >=22 ft, total thermal probability >=0.60, and best thermal class probability >=0.30.
- Other accepted tower/chiller evidence still surfaces as REVIEW.
- On the saved v0.11.3 results this ranking-only change moves 1245 Miller Store and the Coast Guard Station to STRONG, while demoting 1400 Air Rail and 1609 Diamond Springs to REVIEW.

3. Diagnostics
- cv_result.json records deep_rescue_tiles and deep_rescue_verified.
- Deep-rescue detections record both the true Stage-1 candidate score and candidate_scored_as used by the REVIEW-only Stage-2 rescue path.
- prospecting_results.csv includes the new deep-rescue counters.

The detector weights remain frozen. v0.11.4 is a targeted recall/ranking build intended to protect tower/chiller discovery without undoing the property, attribution, residential-prescreen, and packaged-equipment cleanup achieved in v0.11.2-v0.11.3.
