HVAC Territory Discovery v0.11.2
================================

Detector model: unchanged v0.0.12
Thresholds: unchanged 0.07 / 0.35 / 0.45

Field-driven changes:
- Parcel-centered campus overview and high-resolution campus coverage tiles.
- Broader but explicitly labeled campus-adjacent search for true institutional campuses.
- Detection-to-parcel geographic attribution; ordinary parcel tolerance 30 ft.
- Outside-property detections logged/annotated but excluded from ranking.
- Geographic cross-view de-duplication.
- Approximate physical equipment sizing for packaged-HVAC ranking.
- STRONG / REVIEW / QUIET triage.
- Generic Public/Semi Public no longer gets 2,500-ft2 priority exception.
- Town House/Townhome/Townhouse treated as residential.
- Public/self-storage facility names do not inherit an industrial priority exception.
- Prescreen reason shown and exported.
- Persistent user review status/notes in prospecting_results.csv.
- Open Existing Scan allows review to resume after restart.
- view_manifest.json added to each source folder for coverage diagnostics.
